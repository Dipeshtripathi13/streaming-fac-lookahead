RQ2/H2 not supported: manner class is the wrong axis

Tests H2 at the sequence level from the hypothesis dumps -- no audio needed. New
eval/analyse_h2_sequences.py (21 self-tests). Result: a clean negative on a
pre-registered hypothesis.

Relative error-rate reduction L=0 -> 640 ms, native arm, 21 files, 42255
reference phones per lookahead:

  nasal       0.716  (survives)     <- largest gain, wrong group
  approximant 0.700  (breaks-first)
  fricative   0.687  (survives)
  vowel_mono  0.673  (breaks-first)
  vowel_diph  0.654  (breaks-first)
  stop        0.615  (survives)
  affricate   0.525  (survives)

Group means differ in the predicted direction (0.676 vs 0.636) but three harder
checks fail: effect size 0.66 (between-group difference 0.040 < pooled SD
0.060); the survives group's internal spread is 0.190, i.e. 4.7x the
between-group difference; and 5 of 12 pairwise orderings are violated, with nasal
beating all three breaks-first classes. By L1 only 3 of 6 support even the
directional reading, and the gain ratio straddles 1.0 (0.92 Chinese to 1.23
Vietnamese).

The finding is NOT that lookahead fails to help vowels -- every class improves
52-72% relative, all above the 0.0044 noise floor. The finding is that manner
class does not predict which sounds need right context.

A bug nearly produced a fake answer, and it is documented rather than quietly
fixed. phoneme_analysis.CLASS_OF is keyed on ARPAbet (IY, TH, NG); the sweep
emits IPA (i, theta, eng). Applying one to the other sent 8094 of 14085
reference tokens (57%) to class "other", including EVERY vowel and /r/, leaving
breaks_first with l and w alone -- while still printing per-class slopes, an H2
verdict and a by-L1 table. The first run was invalid and looked fine.

Two properties of the transcription then forced context-sensitive mapping, both
verified empirically before being encoded: diphthongs are decomposed (a+I, a+U,
e+I, o+U, O+I) yet the offglides also stand alone -- I follows a diphthong onset
only 38.6% of the time and U 78.5%, so a context-free map would relabel
thousands of genuine monophthongs; and affricates are decomposed (t+S, d+Z), so
without sequence detection the pre-registered affricate class is empty and H2's
survives group silently loses a member.

Guards added: coverage assertion that no declared phone maps to "other", ten
contextual class tests, a reachability test for all seven pre-registered classes,
and a stricter H2 criterion -- direction alone had called a 6% group difference
"supported", so it now also requires effect size >= 1.0 and a minority of
ordering violations. H2_PREDICTION itself is imported unchanged; only the symbol
inventory was translated.

Full write-up in docs/H2_NOT_SUPPORTED.md, including what the audio-domain form
of the test would settle: whether the grouping is wrong or sequence-level PER is
simply blind to formant-trajectory effects.
