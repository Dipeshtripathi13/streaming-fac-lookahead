PRIMARY CATEGORY
----------------
cs.CL     (Computer Science > Computation and Language)

  This is what was used. Both eess.AS and cs.SD required endorsements this
  account did not have; cs.CL did not.

  It is a sound choice on the merits, not only on availability. arXiv defines
  cs.CL as covering natural language processing, ACM class I.2.7, and its own
  scope note names "computational linguistics, speech" explicitly. The
  substance of this paper is phonetic: the probe predicts phone sequences, the
  pre-specified hypothesis is about manner classes, RQ3 turns on canonical
  versus realized phones, and the H2 rationale rests on the phonetics
  literature. cs.SD would have fitted the signal-processing framing; cs.CL
  fits the analysis.
