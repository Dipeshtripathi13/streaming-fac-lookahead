LICENCE
-------

  Pick this one, and note the form's own warning that the choice is
  IRREVOCABLE. The reason to prefer it here is the TASLP submission that
  follows: the arXiv licence grants arXiv what it needs to distribute the
  paper and nothing more, so it does not collide with an IEEE copyright
  transfer later. A CC licence, by contrast, grants the world irrevocable
  reuse rights, which is a commitment you cannot walk back if a publisher
  later asks you not to have made it.

  paper states there was no external funding.
