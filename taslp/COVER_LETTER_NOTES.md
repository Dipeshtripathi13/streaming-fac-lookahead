# Notes on the cover letter

`cover_letter.md` is the letter itself and contains nothing but the letter, so
it can be pasted or attached without editing. These notes are for you and are
not part of the submission.

**Two things to check before pasting.**

1. It opens "Dear Editor-in-Chief". If the portal names a handling editor, use
   their name instead.
2. If arXiv announces before you submit, replace

       A preprint has been submitted to arXiv and is awaiting announcement.

   with

       A preprint of this work is available at arXiv:2609.XXXXX.

   Use the public identifier, not the submission number 8054723, which is an
   internal arXiv tracking number that resolves to nothing.

**What was corrected in this version.** An earlier draft said the manuscript
was "within the ten-page limit for a Regular Paper". That conflated two
different numbers: SPS allows up to 13 double-column pages at initial
submission, while ten is the published length beyond which mandatory
overlength charges of $220 per page begin. The letter now simply states the
length and the paper type.
