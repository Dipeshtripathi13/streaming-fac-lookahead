Highlights

• Per-layer lookahead masks compound with depth and leak future context
• A nominal 40 ms budget used at least 220 ms of future audio in a WavLM probe
• A perturbation test measures the effective future context of a streaming model
• Two masking schemes make the effective context equal the nominal budget
• With exact budgets the lookahead benefit drops from 63% to 3.7-12.5% (PER)
